# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 11, 2026**

We collect your data, sell it to seventeen ad networks, and use the proceeds to fund a surveillance startup.

Just kidding. This plugin changes some text in a config file on your computer. It has no idea you exist. It cannot phone home because it has no home to phone. The only "data" involved is the verb pack you picked, which stays on your machine, where it will live out its days bothering no one.

No telemetry. No analytics. No network requests. No investors to answer to.

For security vulnerabilities or existential concerns, open an issue at https://github.com/AlexanderMcIndoe/spinner-verb-skill/issues.
