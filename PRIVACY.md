# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 11, 2026**

spinner-verbs collects no data. It reads JSON files from your local machine and writes to your local `~/.claude/settings.json`. No information leaves your device. No telemetry, no analytics, no network requests.

For questions or vulnerability reports, open an issue at https://github.com/AlexanderMcIndoe/spinner-verb-skill/issues.
